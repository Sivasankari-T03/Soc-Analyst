Rules explained

R1 — Failure burst then success: N or more failed attempts within W minutes, then success on the same account + computer.

R2 — After-hours success: Successful login outside lab open/close times or on a non-working day. Boundary times count as inside hours.

R3 — Cross-computer activity: Same account appears on ≥2 different computers within the cross-computer window (default 30 min), especially with failures. Catches distributed guessing that R1 misses.

R4 — Slow-burn repeated failures: Many failures spread over a longer period (default ≥8 over 2 hours) without meeting the short R1/R5 burst window. Catches deliberate slow guessing.

R5 — Failure burst without success: N or more failures within W minutes with no subsequent success. No access gained does not mean no threat.

R7 — Password spray shape: Many distinct accounts with few failures each on the same computer within a short window — classic spray pattern.

Risk score (0–100): Deterministic score from matched rules and volume. Optional AI risk summary rephrases facts only — never invents events.

Priority Review: Strong combinations (e.g. R1+R2, R3 with heavy failures, R5 with very high count).

Review / Investigate: Single strong or weaker anomaly patterns.

Routine: Neither rule matches — every routine case still gets a specific reason.

CSV / JSON format

Required fields (names are flexible — column mapping is offered when headers differ):

event_id, timestamp, account, computer, event, department_case

timestamp: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS · event: FAILED or SUCCESS

JSON: array of objects with the same keys (or aliases).

Glossary

Failed login (Windows 4625)
An unsuccessful authentication attempt.
Successful login (Windows 4624)
A successful authentication.
Brute force (MITRE T1110)
Repeated password guessing; LabTrace surfaces short bursts for review.
Valid accounts (MITRE T1078)
Use of legitimate credentials; after-hours success may still warrant a check.
False positive
A flagged case that a human later dismisses as expected.
Human in the loop
LabTrace never acts alone — a person confirms or dismisses every alert.
Synthetic data
The sample dataset is fabricated for the prototype; it is not real lab traffic.

FAQ

Does it block accounts? No. LabTrace never blocks, locks, disables or bans anyone.

Why is department not used? department_case is a context label for the human only. The rule engine never reads it.

What does it miss? Very slow attacks, credential use that looks completely normal, or activity outside the imported logs.

Is data sent anywhere? No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing leaves this machine.
